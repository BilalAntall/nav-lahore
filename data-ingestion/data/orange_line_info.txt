1. System Overview and General Information
The Orange Line Metro Train (OLMT), internally designated as line ID "olmt_lahore", is an automated rapid transit line in Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan. It is the first metro train system in the country, spanning approximately 27.1 kilometers. The system uses an official color code of "#FF8C00" (Dark Orange). The line consists of 26 stations in total, stretching from Ali Town in the south-west of Lahore to Dera Gujran in the north-east. Out of the 26 stations, 24 are elevated and 2 are underground to preserve historical sites. The Orange Line serves as a critical transport corridor along Multan Road, McLeod Road, and the Grand Trunk (GT) Road, connecting significant residential, commercial, educational, and historical zones of Lahore.
2. Comprehensive Station Profiles
Station ID 1: Ali Town Station (Southwestern Terminal)
Basic Details: Ali Town is Station Number 1 on the Orange Line Metro Train. It serves as the Southwestern Terminal of the system. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.465066 and longitude 74.243953.
Surrounding Area and Geography: As the southwestern anchor of the line, Ali Town station is situated near Raiwind Road and acts as a major hub for commuters coming from the deep southern suburbs of Lahore, including Bahria Town, Tariq Gardens, and Valencia.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: This station provides critical access to students and professionals traveling into the city center from the sprawling southern housing societies. Passengers looking to travel towards the city center, Anarkali, or GT Road will begin their journey here. It does not have a direct internal interchange but connects with local feeder buses, auto-rickshaws, and ride-hailing services waiting at the Raiwind Road junction.
Station ID 2: Thokar Niaz Baig Station
Basic Details: Thokar Niaz Baig is Station Number 2 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.475803 and longitude 74.242816.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Thokar Niaz Baig is one of the most important geographical chokepoints and entry points into Lahore. The station is located near the M2 Motorway Interchange, acting as the primary gateway for travelers arriving from Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and southern Punjab.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The station overlooks the massive Jinnah Bus Terminal, where intra-city and inter-city buses operate. Travelers arriving in Lahore via the M2 motorway or Multan Road use this station to bypass heavy city traffic and reach the inner city. The surrounding area includes the Bab-e-Pakistan monument and various commercial plazas. Commuters often ask chatbots about this station when transitioning from inter-city buses to the local metro.
Station ID 3: Canal View Station
Basic Details: Canal View is Station Number 3 on the Lahore Orange Line. It is an Elevated station positioned at latitude 31.482624 and longitude 74.251292.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Located adjacent to the picturesque Lahore Canal, this station serves the upscale and well-planned residential neighborhood of Canal View Housing Society. The Lahore Canal is a central geographical feature of the city, lined with green belts and trees.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The station serves residents of Canal View, Tech Society, and nearby parts of Johar Town. It provides a scenic view of the canal from its elevated platforms. Students and professionals residing in these middle-to-upper-class residential sectors rely heavily on this station to commute towards the city center without dealing with the traffic congestion along the canal road.
Station ID 4: Hanjarwal Station
Basic Details: Hanjarwal is Station Number 4 on the Orange Line Metro. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.491179 and longitude 74.261752.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Situated directly on Multan Road, Hanjarwal is a densely populated, mixed-use commercial and residential area. The station serves as a bridge between the highly urbanized older colonies and the newer developments along Multan Road.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: Nearby landmarks include the back-end access to the University of the Punjab (Quaid-e-Azam Campus) and Mansoora, the headquarters of the Jamaat-e-Islami political party. Shoppers, local workers, and university students frequently use this station. The surrounding commercial zone is packed with local markets, auto-repair shops, and small-scale industries.
Station ID 5: Wahdat Road Station
Basic Details: Wahdat Road is Station Number 5 on the Orange Line system. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.494614 and longitude 74.263114.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Wahdat Road is a major arterial road in Lahore that intersects with Multan Road at this station's location. The road extends eastward toward Ferozepur Road.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: Wahdat Road is famous for its massive concentration of educational institutions, including Government Science College, Punjab University testing centers, and various public schools. Therefore, Wahdat Road station experiences heavy student traffic during morning and early afternoon hours. It also serves the residential blocks of Awan Town and Mustafa Town on the western flank.
Station ID 6: Awan Town Station
Basic Details: Awan Town is Station Number 6 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station with coordinates at latitude 31.503332 and longitude 74.268108.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Awan Town is a prominent residential neighborhood bordering Multan Road. The station serves the local inhabitants of Awan Town, Hassan Town, and adjacent densely packed housing schemes.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: This station primarily serves daily commuters—shopkeepers, office workers, and families traveling toward Anarkali or GT Road. The area surrounding the station is characterized by narrow residential streets feeding into the main Multan Road, with high localized commercial activity including grocery stores, bakeries, and community mosques.
Station ID 7: Sabzazar Station
Basic Details: Sabzazar is Station Number 7 on the Orange Line Metro Train. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.510490 and longitude 74.272985.
Surrounding Area and Geography: The station acts as the primary transit point for the Sabzazar Housing Scheme, a large planned residential community managed by the Lahore Development Authority (LDA).
Points of Interest and Connectivity: Sabzazar is known for its wide main boulevards and numerous parks. The station is a crucial drop-off for residents of Sabzazar and nearby Saidpur. It helps alleviate the massive traffic bottlenecks that traditionally occur on Multan Road near the Sabzazar entrance. It is heavily utilized by local commuters heading to the industrial areas further north or the commercial hubs in the east.
Station ID 8: Khatam-e-Nabuwat Station (Formerly Shahnoor)
Basic Details: Khatam-e-Nabuwat is Station Number 8 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.517825 and longitude 74.277645. It is officially noted that the former name of this station was "Shahnoor".
Surrounding Area and Geography: The station is located on Multan Road. The former name, Shahnoor, references the historic Shahnoor Studios located nearby, which was once the beating heart of Lollywood (the Pakistani film industry) founded by Noor Jehan and Shaukat Hussain Rizvi.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The station was renamed to Khatam-e-Nabuwat for religious and cultural significance. The surrounding area remains a mix of historical media sites, dense residential blocks, and commercial markets. Commuters looking for the historic film studios, or residents of the nearby densely populated inner-city suburbs, use this station.
Station ID 9: Salahuddin Road Station
Basic Details: Salahuddin Road is Station Number 9 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.524474 and longitude 74.283248.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Positioned further up Multan Road as it approaches the older parts of the city, this station serves an intensely busy commercial district.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The neighborhood is a maze of wholesale markets, timber yards, and residential colonies. Salahuddin Road itself connects deep into local neighborhoods. The station is highly beneficial for local traders, laborers, and shoppers who historically struggled with the lack of parking and high traffic density of Multan Road.
Station ID 10: Bund Road Station
Basic Details: Bund Road is Station Number 10 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.532467 and longitude 74.287545.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Bund Road is one of Lahore’s most significant bypass roads, running roughly parallel to the Ravi River and connecting the southern parts of the city with the northern reaches and the Ring Road.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The intersection of Multan Road and Bund Road is a massive transportation hub, featuring numerous inter-city bus stands catering to southern Punjab, Sindh, and Balochistan. The station serves as a crucial interchange node for travelers disembarking from long-distance buses (like Daewoo, Faisal Movers) who need to access the city center. Expect heavy luggage-carrying passengers and a bustling, chaotic atmosphere beneath the station.
Station ID 11: Samanabad Station
Basic Details: Samanabad is Station Number 11 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.539935 and longitude 74.293444.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Samanabad is one of Lahore's oldest planned residential neighborhoods, known for its circular roads, parks (like Doongi Ground), and established middle-class communities.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The station is near the famous Samanabad Morr (intersection) on Multan Road. The area is famous for its vibrant street food scene, particularly at Ghazali Road. Commuters using this station include generational residents of Samanabad, students attending local government colleges, and food enthusiasts.
Station ID 12: Gulshan-e-Ravi Station
Basic Details: Gulshan-e-Ravi is Station Number 12 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station positioned at latitude 31.545283 and longitude 74.294975.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Gulshan-e-Ravi is a massive residential scheme branching off from Multan Road towards the west. The station serves as the gateway to this expansive housing area.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The neighborhood is famous for the Moon Market, a massive commercial and shopping district known for clothing, eateries, and family shopping. Residents of Gulshan-e-Ravi no longer need to rely on congested local transport to reach downtown Lahore, making this station highly active during morning and evening rush hours.
Station ID 13: Chauburji Station
Basic Details: Chauburji is Station Number 13 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.552300 and longitude 74.302900.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Chauburji is a profoundly historic location. The station overlooks the famous Chauburji monument, a 17th-century Mughal-era gateway with four minarets. It marks the geographical point where Multan Road officially begins its journey out of the central city.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The construction of the elevated track around the Chauburji monument was a feat of engineering, specifically curved and elevated to protect the UNESCO-recognized heritage site. The station serves tourists visiting the monument, as well as the dense commercial populations of Lower Mall, Bahawalpur Road, and Lake Road. This is a critical station bridging the modern city and the historic downtown.
Station ID 14: Anarkali Station (Interchange Hub)
Basic Details: Anarkali is Station Number 14 on the Orange Line Metro Train. Notably, it is an Underground station, located at latitude 31.567329 and longitude 74.309399.
Surrounding Area and Geography: The station went underground to protect the highly sensitive historical fabric of the area. It is located near the world-famous Anarkali Bazaar, one of the oldest surviving markets in South Asia, dating back over 200 years.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: Crucial RAG Data: Anarkali Station is an Interchange station. It connects directly to the Lahore Metro Bus System (Green Line) at the MAO College transit station via a pedestrian walkway. This allows commuters to switch between the East-West Orange Line and the North-South Metro Bus without exiting the rapid transit network. The station is heavily used by shoppers heading to Anarkali Bazaar, students of the Punjab University Old Campus, National College of Arts (NCA), and Government College University (GCU).
Station ID 15: Central (GPO) Station
Basic Details: Central Station, commonly referred to as GPO, is Station Number 15. It is an Underground station located at latitude 31.570839 and longitude 74.315922.
Surrounding Area and Geography: The station is located at the historic Mall & McLeod Intersection. It is buried beneath the General Post Office (GPO) intersection, adjacent to the historic Mall Road (Shahrah-e-Quaid-e-Azam).
Points of Interest and Connectivity: This station is the cultural and administrative heart of Lahore. Nearby landmarks include the Lahore High Court, the State Bank of Pakistan, the General Post Office (a beautiful British-colonial building), and the highly commercial Hall Road (the electronics hub of Punjab). It does not have an official transit interchange, but its central location makes it the most critical stop for lawyers, judges, bankers, and shoppers visiting the historic Mall Road.
Station ID 16: Lakshmi Station
Basic Details: Lakshmi is Station Number 16 on the Orange Line system. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.572594 and longitude 74.326909.
Surrounding Area and Geography: As the line emerges from the underground section, it arrives at the iconic Lakshmi Chowk on McLeod Road. Lakshmi Chowk is the undisputed culinary heart of traditional Lahore.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The area is world-renowned for its traditional foods, particularly Karahi, Hareesa, and BBQ. Additionally, Lakshmi Chowk was the historic center of the Pakistani cinema industry, surrounded by historic single-screen cinemas. The station serves food tourists, locals going out for dinner, and traders from the nearby Royal Park area (famous for printing and paper wholesale).
Station ID 17: Railway Station (Interchange Hub)
Basic Details: Railway Station is Station Number 17 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.576104 and longitude 74.334805.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Located in the Naulakha area, this station stands proudly next to the majestic Lahore Junction (Lahore Railway Station), a sprawling colonial-era brick building that serves as the headquarters of Pakistan Railways.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: Crucial RAG Data: This is a major Interchange station. It provides direct connectivity to Pakistan Railways (for inter-city train travel across Pakistan) and the Eco-Bus transit system. Commuters from all over Pakistan arriving in Lahore via train use this station to immediately access the Orange Line to travel deep into the city without needing taxis. The surrounding area includes dense historical bazaars like Landa Bazaar (famous for imported second-hand clothing) and Circular Road leading to the Walled City.
Station ID 18: Sultanpura Station
Basic Details: Sultanpura is Station Number 18 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.576543 and longitude 74.352744.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Moving eastward, the line enters the heavily populated, older working-class neighborhoods of northern Lahore. Sultanpura is located near the Misri Shah area, which is famous as the largest scrap iron and metal market in Asia.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The station primarily serves local residents and metal market workers. The streets here are incredibly dense, and before the Orange Line, residents faced immense difficulties traversing the city due to traffic congestion involving cargo trucks and rickshaws. The elevated station provides a rapid, clean escape from the chaotic streets below.
Station ID 19: UET Station
Basic Details: UET is Station Number 19 on the Orange Line Metro Train. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.577640 and longitude 74.359975.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Located on the historic Grand Trunk (GT) Road, this station is named after and situated directly outside the University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Lahore, one of the oldest and most prestigious engineering institutions in Pakistan.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The primary user base of this station consists of thousands of engineering students, university faculty, and staff. It heavily decongests the GT road traffic. The surrounding area also includes various hostels, student cafes, and bookshops tailored to the university demographic.
Station ID 20: Baghbanpura Station
Basic Details: Baghbanpura is Station Number 20 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.579687 and longitude 74.370682.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Continuing along the GT Road, the line passes through Baghbanpura, a historic neighborhood known for its deep roots dating back to the Mughal era. It was historically the residential area for the caretakers ("Baghbans") of the royal gardens.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: Baghbanpura is a bustling, highly congested commercial and residential mix. The local bazaar is immensely popular for everyday shopping. The station provides a lifeline to the inner-city residents who travel westward towards the city center or eastward towards the industrial zones for work.
Station ID 21: Shalamar Garden Station
Basic Details: Shalamar Garden is Station Number 21 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.583801 and longitude 74.380058.
Surrounding Area and Geography: This is one of the most tourist-heavy stations on the network. It is located right next to the magnificent Shalimar Gardens (Shalamar Bagh), a UNESCO World Heritage Site built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in 1641.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: Like Chauburji, the structural design of the Orange Line here faced strict UNESCO scrutiny to ensure the vibrations and visual lines did not damage the historical integrity of the Mughal gardens. The station serves thousands of domestic and international tourists visiting the cascading fountains and terraced gardens of Shalimar, as well as the locals of GT Road.
Station ID 22: Pakistan Mint Station
Basic Details: Pakistan Mint is Station Number 22 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.585415 and longitude 74.389057.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Located further east on GT Road, the station is named after the Pakistan Mint, the country's official coin-making facility, which is a highly secure and historic installation operating since 1942.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The station serves the employees of the Mint, the adjacent residential colony of Mint employees, and the heavily industrial and commercial populations of eastern GT Road. The area marks a transition from historical Lahore to the industrial outskirts of the city.
Station ID 23: Mahmood Booti Station
Basic Details: Mahmood Booti is Station Number 23 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.586914 and longitude 74.402392.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Mahmood Booti was historically known as a massive landfill and waste management area on the periphery of the city, but has since seen substantial urban development and integration with the Lahore Ring Road.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The station is located near the Mahmood Booti Interchange of the Lahore Ring Road. It provides excellent connectivity for commuters looking to transfer between the Metro Train and vehicles traveling via the high-speed Ring Road to the airport or DHA. It serves the surrounding suburban and newly developed industrial populations.
Station ID 24: Salamatpura Station
Basic Details: Salamatpura is Station Number 24 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.588067 and longitude 74.412750.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Salamatpura is primarily an industrial and working-class residential area on the eastern edge of Lahore along GT Road.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: The area is characterized by factories, warehouses, and small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs). The station is predominantly used by blue-collar workers, factory supervisors, and local suburban residents commuting into the central city. The availability of rapid transit here has revolutionized the commute for the industrial workforce.
Station ID 25: Islam Park Station
Basic Details: Islam Park is Station Number 25 on the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.588433 and longitude 74.420100.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Islam Park is a suburban residential neighborhood located near the very end of the Orange Line track. It represents the eastward expansion of Lahore's housing footprint along the GT Road.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: This station primarily serves localized residential traffic. It acts as an overflow and penultimate stop before the eastern terminal. Commuters from nearby smaller settlements and rural-urban fringe villages use this station to access the modern city.
Station ID 26: Dera Gujran Station (Northeastern Terminal)
Basic Details: Dera Gujran is Station Number 26 and serves as the Northeastern Terminal of the Orange Line. It is an Elevated station located at latitude 31.589837 and longitude 74.431013.
Surrounding Area and Geography: Located near the Quaid-e-Azam Interchange of the Lahore Ring Road and the GT Road boundary, Dera Gujran marks the end of the line.
Points of Interest and Connectivity: Crucial RAG Data: The main Train Depot for the Orange Line Metro is located here at Dera Gujran, where the trains are parked, washed, and maintained. The station serves incoming traffic from the Wagah border area, Jallo Park, and travelers entering Lahore from the GT road (from cities like Gujranwala or Muridke). Feeder buses and rickshaws connect arriving passengers to rural and suburban eastern districts.
