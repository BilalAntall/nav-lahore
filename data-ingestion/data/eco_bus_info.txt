Geographic Alignment: Central to South-West Lahore (Lahore Railway Station to Township/Minhaj University)
OVERVIEW
The Electro Eco-Bus (Route 1) is a critical surface-level transit artery in Lahore, Pakistan, operating environmentally friendly electric buses. The route diagonally bisects the city, starting from the dense, historic transportation hub of the Lahore Railway Station in the northeast, weaving through the colonial and political heart of the city (Mall Road, Charing Cross), moving through high-density commercial zones (Abid Market, Ichhra), crossing the educational sector (Punjab University), and finally terminating in the southern middle-class and industrial-adjacent residential blocks of Township. This document provides extreme detail on every station to aid in spatial, historical, and contextual queries.
STATION PROFILES
1. Railway Station (Haji Camp)
Station ID: 1
Coordinates: Lat 31.576104, Lng 74.334805
Type: Surface
Interchange: True
Surrounding Area & Context:
This station serves as the northern terminus and a massive multimodal transit hub. It is situated adjacent to the Lahore Junction Railway Station, a grand fortress-like colonial structure built during the British Raj. The specific designation "Haji Camp" refers to the nearby staging grounds historically used by pilgrims departing for the Hajj in Saudi Arabia. The area is a high-chaos, high-volume environment filled with budget hotels, intercity bus aggregators, railway logistics warehouses, and cargo offices. The demographic is heavily transient, consisting of travelers from all over Pakistan. It is a critical interchange connecting the Eco-Bus network to national rail lines and local auto-rickshaw networks.
2. Police Lines
Station ID: 2
Coordinates: Lat 31.571420, Lng 74.336210
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Located near the Qila Gujjar Singh area, this station serves the administrative and security epicenter of local law enforcement. The District Police Lines (Qila Gujjar Singh Police Station) is a massive compound housing police barracks, training grounds, and administrative offices for the Punjab Police. The immediate surroundings feature a heavy security presence, legal chambers, uniform supply stores, and local tea stalls (dhabas) catering to off-duty officers and civilians navigating the legal/police bureaucracy. The neighborhood behind the main road is an old, densely packed residential zone.
3. Shimla Pahari
Station ID: 3
Coordinates: Lat 31.564481, Lng 74.339230
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Shimla Pahari (Shimla Hill) is one of Lahore's most famous geographic landmarks and intersections, where Egerton Road, Empress Road, and Durand Road converge around a raised, pine-tree-lined roundabout. The station is the nucleus of Lahore's media and press industry. Overlooking the station is the Lahore Press Club and the regional headquarters of Pakistan Television (PTV), as well as Radio Pakistan. The area is frequently the site of civil protests, journalistic gatherings, and political rallies. It features colonial-era bungalows now repurposed into corporate offices and government administrative blocks.
4. Governor House
Station ID: 4
Coordinates: Lat 31.558231, Lng 74.331545
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Situated on the iconic Mall Road (Shahrah-e-Quaid-e-Azam), this station sits outside the sprawling, highly fortified walls of the Punjab Governor House. The area exudes colonial grandeur, featuring wide, tree-canopied boulevards and impeccably maintained landscaping. Just a short walk away is the Alhamra Arts Council, making this a culturally significant stop. The demographics here shift toward government officials, artists, and tourists. The station area is subject to heavy VIP movement and frequent security cordons.
5. Bagh-e-Jinnah
Station ID: 5
Coordinates: Lat 31.552912, Lng 74.330101
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Serving the eastern gates of Bagh-e-Jinnah (formerly Lawrence Gardens), this station drops commuters at the edge of Lahore's largest and most historic botanical park. The 141-acre green space houses the magnificent Quaid-e-Azam Library (a Victorian-era architectural masterpiece), botanical research institutes, open-air theaters, and tennis clubs. It is a highly serene, lush environment. The primary users of this station are university students, morning joggers, families seeking recreation, and researchers visiting the library.
6. Lahore Zoo
Station ID: 6
Coordinates: Lat 31.555891, Lng 74.325876
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Located just down the road from Bagh-e-Jinnah, this station serves the main entrance of the Lahore Zoo, one of the oldest zoos in South Asia (established in 1872). This station experiences massive spikes in foot traffic during weekends, school holidays, and Eid festivals. The surrounding pavement is usually bustling with balloon vendors, face-painters, and street food carts catering to families and children. Across the road, the architectural landscape features heritage buildings and upscale, old-money residential properties.
7. Charing Cross
Station ID: 7
Coordinates: Lat 31.555102, Lng 74.321455
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Charing Cross (officially Faisal Square) is the political and corporate heart of Punjab. The station is surrounded by a breathtaking panorama of monumental architecture: the majestic Punjab Provincial Assembly building, the towering WAPDA House (headquarters of the national power utility), the Islamic Summit Minar, and the historic Alfalah Building. This station is the definitive epicenter for major political demonstrations and sit-ins in Lahore. It serves civil servants, politicians, corporate executives, and bankers.
8. Ganga Ram Hospital
Station ID: 8
Coordinates: Lat 31.556214, Lng 74.315900
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Located near the intersection of Queens Road, this station is primarily a medical transit node. It serves Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, one of Lahore's largest and most historic public healthcare facilities, and the adjoining Fatima Jinnah Medical University (a premier all-women's medical college). The area is highly congested with ambulances, patients, medical students, and an endless array of private pharmacies, diagnostic laboratories, and surgical supply stores. The atmosphere is urgent and bustling 24/7.
9. Waris Road
Station ID: 9
Coordinates: Lat 31.550123, Lng 74.316220
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Waris Road is a historic, mixed-use street that connects Queens Road to Jail Road. It is characterized by old colonial-style homes, some of which have been converted into private clinics, boutiques, and educational centers. The station serves a quiet but dense residential community, alongside several historic churches and Christian schools situated in the neighborhood. It acts as a transitional, quieter node before the route plunges into heavy commercial zones.
10. Abid Market
Station ID: 10
Coordinates: Lat 31.545890, Lng 74.317550
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Abid Market, located near Temple Road, is arguably the largest and most famous electronics and home appliance market in Punjab. The station is engulfed by massive neon signs advertising air conditioners, refrigerators, televisions, and mobile phones. The sidewalks are heavily encroached upon by boxed appliances and retail displays. The area is a massive draw for families setting up new homes or purchasing dowry items. The traffic here is notoriously slow due to loading trucks and double-parked vehicles.
11. Mozang
Station ID: 11
Coordinates: Lat 31.543210, Lng 74.318990
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Mozang (or Mozang Chungi) is one of the oldest settlements in Lahore, pre-dating the colonial era. Today, it is a highly dense, culturally rich residential and commercial maze. Mozang is regionally famous for its food culture, specifically "Mozang Fish" (fried fish) and traditional BBQ spots that operate late into the night. The station serves the working and lower-middle-class residents of the inner neighborhoods. The architecture is tightly packed, with narrow alleys spider-webbing away from the main road.
12. Ichhra
Station ID: 12
Coordinates: Lat 31.530932, Lng 74.327575
Type: Surface
Interchange: True
Connects To: Metro Bus (Green Line)
Surrounding Area & Context:
This is a critical interchange station, allowing passengers to switch between the Electro Eco-Bus and the elevated Metro Bus (Green Line) on Ferozepur Road. Ichhra is legendary for its massive traditional bazaar. Shoppers flock here from all over the province for wholesale unstitched fabric, bridal wear, jewelry, and household goods. The sheer volume of pedestrians, localized public transport, and street hawkers makes it one of the most hyper-active, loud, and economically vibrant nodes in Lahore.
13. Punjab College (Canal)
Station ID: 13
Coordinates: Lat 31.517890, Lng 74.332100
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Located near the Lahore Canal in the Muslim Town area, this station serves the massive educational ecosystem belonging to the Punjab Group of Colleges (PGC). The demographic here is overwhelmingly young—thousands of intermediate and undergraduate students use this stop daily. The surrounding area features numerous student hostels, stationary shops, local cafes, and academies. The tree-lined canal provides a scenic backdrop to an otherwise heavily trafficked, youthful, and energetic transit point.
14. PU Campus Bridge (Gate 4)
Station ID: 14
Coordinates: Lat 31.503212, Lng 74.307185
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
This station is situated at the crucial Gate 4 access point of the University of the Punjab (Quaid-e-Azam New Campus). The campus itself is akin to a small city, featuring expansive forests, internal bus routes, and massive academic blocks. This station drops students directly at the pedestrian bridge over the canal that links the campus to the bordering residential areas of Garden Town. The vibe is highly academic, characterized by student political banners, book stalls, and tea cafes.
15. Hailey College / IBA
Station ID: 15
Coordinates: Lat 31.498110, Lng 74.305220
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Located deep within the university/academic belt, this station specifically serves two of Punjab University's most prestigious institutions: Hailey College of Commerce and the Institute of Business Administration (IBA). The surrounding environment is characterized by wide, leafy campus roads, student dormitories, massive sports grounds, and academic research centers. The station ensures seamless transit for thousands of business and commerce students without them having to rely on expensive private transport.
16. Shah Di Khoi
Station ID: 16
Coordinates: Lat 31.491220, Lng 74.302550
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Historically an independent rural village ("Khoi" translating to "well"), Shah Di Khoi has been completely subsumed by the urban sprawl of Johar Town and the expanding university campuses. Today, it acts as a transitional neighborhood catering heavily to the student population. The area is packed with private student hostels, affordable local eateries, internet cafes, and laundry services. It bridges the academic atmosphere of the university with the fast-paced commercialization of Johar Town.
17. Ravi Chowk
Station ID: 17
Coordinates: Lat 31.485550, Lng 74.304880
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Ravi Chowk is a bustling local intersection serving as a connective tissue between the southern edges of Garden Town/Johar Town and the approaching Township area. The surrounding geography is a mix of middle-class residential homes, local grocery markets, and mid-tier healthcare clinics. It marks the shift from the academic epicenter of Lahore into the southern suburban/commercial grid.
18. Akbar Chowk
Station ID: 18
Coordinates: Lat 31.481905, Lng 74.310112
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Akbar Chowk is a major intersection and commercial hub located on Maulana Shaukat Ali Road, separating Faisal Town from Township. The station is surrounded by immense commercial activity: fast-food chains, massive clothing retail outlets, bakeries, and banks. In the evenings, the area transforms into a vibrant local food street. It is a critical drop-off point for shoppers and retail workers in the southern districts of Lahore.
19. Township Bazaar
Station ID: 19
Coordinates: Lat 31.470220, Lng 74.308550
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
This station plunges into the heart of Township's central commercial sector. Township Bazaar is a dense, highly frequented market serving the vast middle-and-working-class populations of southern Lahore. The bazaar is renowned for affordable clothing, unstitched fabrics, local tailors, shoe stores, and fresh produce markets. The road is incredibly congested with rickshaws, motorcycles, and pedestrians, reflecting a highly localized, community-driven economy.
20. Honda Mor
Station ID: 20
Coordinates: Lat 31.461110, Lng 74.304220
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Locally known as "Honda Mor" (Honda Turn) due to a historically prominent Honda auto-dealership and service center at the junction. This area marks the transition from residential Township into the semi-industrial and hardware-focused zones bordering Kot Lakhpat. The immediate vicinity features auto repair workshops, spare parts dealers, hardware stores, and small-scale manufacturing units.
21. Pindi Stop
Station ID: 21
Coordinates: Lat 31.455660, Lng 74.302110
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
Pindi Stop is a well-known local landmark in the Township/Green Town boundary area. It serves a very dense, working-class residential neighborhood. The station is surrounded by informal markets, street-side vegetable vendors, local barbershops, and utility stores. It is heavily utilized by blue-collar workers and daily wage laborers commuting to the nearby Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate.
22. Hamdard Chowk (Depot)
Station ID: 22
Coordinates: Lat 31.445220, Lng 74.301550
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Surrounding Area & Context:
This station is critical for the logistics of the Eco-Bus system, as it sits adjacent to a major transit bus depot where vehicles are parked, charged, and maintained. Hamdard Chowk connects Township to Green Town. The area is highly industrial, sitting on the fringes of the Kot Lakhpat industrial zone. Massive factories producing textiles, electronics, and consumer goods are located nearby. The air quality and noise levels here reflect its industrial nature, and the station is highly trafficked by factory shift workers.
23. Minhaj University
Station ID: 23
Coordinates: Lat 31.441220, Lng 74.301110
Type: Surface
Interchange: False
Notes: Final Stop
Surrounding Area & Context:
The southern terminal of the Electro Eco-Bus (Route 1) serves Minhaj University Lahore, an expansive higher education institute established by the Minhaj-ul-Quran organization. Located in the Township/Madina Town area, the station acts as the daily drop-off for thousands of students and faculty members. The immediate surroundings have evolved to cater to this demographic, featuring bookshops, student cafes, printing presses, and private dormitories. It provides a peaceful, academic bookend to a route that traverses the chaotic heart of Lahore.
